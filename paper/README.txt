Perfect play with spatial quantum strategies is Sigma^0_2-complete
Seth Douglas and Nidhal Mghirbi
Version 1.1.0, November 2026

Build: python build.py
Requires Python 3, pdflatex, BibTeX and the LaTeX packages named in main.tex.
main.pdf is the compiled preprint. main.tex inputs sections/*.tex.
references.bib uses the plain bibliography style. abstract.txt is plain text.
The sibling verification/ directory describes the finite checks and their limits.
No publication or submission is performed by the build.
