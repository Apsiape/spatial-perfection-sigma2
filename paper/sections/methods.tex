\section*{How this work was done}\label{sec:methods}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{How this work was done}

The authors selected the questions and proof standards, directed the research, and take responsibility for every claim. AI systems from more than one developer assisted with exploration, derivation, programming and drafting.

The mathematical evidence consists of the proofs in this paper and the explicitly cited external theorems. The ancillary programs provide reproducible finite checks. Before the rewrite, review by a different model family checked the underlying derivations of the upper bound, compiler, rooted computation, clock, reduction, value-one argument and Bell attainment. The final manuscript was audited separately; the present revision addresses that manuscript audit. The finite programs test truncated tree and clock relations, a pulse-reading front, good primitive phases, and the compiler ingredients described in Appendix~\ref{sec:repro}. Their arithmetic and scope are stated there; numerical checks are distinguished from exact checks.

These finite checks do not prove the infinite-dimensional compactness argument, the general reduction, or an unrestricted robustness theorem by sampling. The asymptotic conclusions rest on the written proofs. This preprint has not been peer reviewed.
